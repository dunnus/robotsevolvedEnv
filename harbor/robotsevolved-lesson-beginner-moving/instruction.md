Solve the locally stored Robots Evolved **Moving** lesson. Robots slide north (`N`), east (`E`), south (`S`), or west (`W`) until a wall or another robot stops them. Robot letters are `B`, `G`, `R`, and `Y`; any robot may stop on the wildcard goal.

Use only the offline helper: `robot-game show` displays the board and `robot-game test RE,RS,...` tests a candidate. Write a JSON array of no more than 20 moves to `/workspace/solution.json`. Do not modify the simulator or puzzle.

Lesson hint: select a robot, move it to line up with the goal, and use walls to stop it.
